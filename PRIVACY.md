# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

SonicVault ("the App") is an open-source music client. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles data.

## Data Collection

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Analytics or crash reporting

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**. This data is never transmitted to our servers:

- **Music preferences** (playlists, history, library) — stored in a local database
- **App settings** — stored in local preferences
- **Login credentials** for third-party services (YouTube, Spotify, Last.fm) — stored in encrypted local storage

### Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the following third-party services. Your use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies:

- **YouTube Music** — for music streaming and metadata. Subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Spotify** — for music metadata and lyrics. Subject to [Spotify's Privacy Policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
- **Last.fm** — for scrobbling and music recommendations (optional, requires user configuration). Subject to [Last.fm's Privacy Policy](https://www.last.fm/legal/privacy).
- **Shazam** — for music recognition (optional). Subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://privacy.apple.com/).

### GMS Build Only
The Google Mobile Services (GMS) build variant may include:
- **Start.io** advertising SDK for optional support ads. This may collect advertising identifiers. Subject to [Start.io's Privacy Policy](https://www.start.io/privacy/).

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your data. All sensitive credentials are stored using Android's encrypted storage mechanisms.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the App's repository and take effect upon publication.
